Smile Dental Clinic - Patient Information

Opening Hours
Smile Dental Clinic is open from Saturday to Thursday, from 10 AM to 9 PM. The clinic is closed on Fridays and on official public holidays. During Ramadan, the clinic works from 8 PM to 1 AM.

Location
The clinic is located at 15 Tahrir Street, Dokki, Giza, on the second floor above Misr Pharmacy. Free parking is available behind the building.

Prices
A dental checkup costs 300 EGP. A teeth cleaning session costs 500 EGP. Teeth whitening costs 2500 EGP. A tooth extraction costs 800 EGP. A filling costs 600 EGP. A root canal treatment costs 2000 EGP. A dental implant costs 15000 EGP per tooth.

Our Doctors
Dr. Ahmed Samir is our implant specialist with 15 years of experience. Dr. Mai Hassan specializes in children's dentistry and is known for making kids feel comfortable. Dr. Omar Fathy handles orthodontics and braces.

Payment
The clinic accepts Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards, and cash. Installment plans are available for implants and braces through valU.

Booking and Cancellation
To book an appointment, call 01000000000 or send a WhatsApp message to the same number. Please cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations may be charged 100 EGP.
